Before we get started

A few good
questions.

How long does a website take?

A simple site is planned around 2–3 weeks; a larger site around 4–6 weeks after content and scope are ready. These are planning estimates. We agree a schedule based on your project and feedback availability.

Can you refresh my existing website?

Yes. Refresh & Overhaul covers anything from styling updates to a full redesign. We review your existing site and agree the pages, platform, changes and price before starting.

Can you connect my booking system?

Yes. I can connect visitors to an existing service such as GlossGenius through clear booking links. Embedded booking depends on what your provider supports. Booking software subscriptions and custom booking systems are separate.

Will I own my website?

The agreement will cover transfer of the custom work after final payment. Your domain and service accounts should stay in your name. Third-party assets, fonts and platforms remain subject to their own licenses and terms.

What happens after launch?

Your package includes 14 days of support for issues with the agreed build. Ongoing content changes can be handled through Website Care or quoted as needed. A new feature or additional page is scoped separately.

What do you need from me?

Your goals, approved text, logo, photos, service details and any existing booking links. We’ll identify missing pieces before setting the schedule. Branding, copywriting and photography can be discussed as separate work.
