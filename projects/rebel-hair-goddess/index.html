Rebel Hair Goddess

A salon website with a distinct personality and a straightforward route from inspiration to booking.

Visit the live website ↗ (opens in a new tab)

The business

Rebel Hair Goddess is a salon business in Wheaton, Illinois. Its website brings the brand, stylist introduction, service information, and booking journey together in one cohesive experience.

The design

The website uses a strong headline, intentional photography, and a warm palette to establish the brand’s tone. A short navigation menu keeps the focus on the stylist, services, and booking rather than competing calls to action.

Project details

A recognizable first impression

The salon name and “Good hair lives here” message set the tone as soon as visitors arrive.

Easy-to-explore services

A concise service overview links through to more detailed service information.

Booking within reach

Repeated booking links connect visitors to the salon’s existing appointment platform.
