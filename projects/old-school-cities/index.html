Old School Cities

A city-inspired apparel identity built around familiar places, local stories, and a sense of another era.

Explore the project ↗ (opens in a new tab)

The business

Old School Cities is Ryan’s independent apparel brand project. Its direction brings together vintage city culture and illustrated storytelling under the line “Same City. Different Era. Better Stories.”

The design

A skyline emblem anchors the identity, with cream, charcoal, and gold establishing a restrained visual palette. The presentation gives the brand a recognizable starting point for an online presence and future collections.

Project details

A skyline-led identity

The emblem makes the city theme visible without relying on long explanations.

A consistent visual palette

Muted tones support the brand’s vintage direction across its presentation.

A personal brand project

This is an independent brand exploration, presented separately from client website work.
