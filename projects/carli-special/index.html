Carli Special

A personal salon experience, translated into a website that makes services and booking easy to explore.

Visit the live website ↗ (opens in a new tab)

The business

Carli Special is an independent hair color studio in Wheaton, Illinois. The website introduces Carli’s approach, explains color services, and helps visitors take the next step toward an appointment.

The design

The design pairs editorial photography with restrained typography and a clear page structure. Mobile navigation and prominent booking links keep the experience practical for visitors browsing from their phones.

Project details

Services with context

Service descriptions, starting prices, and appointment durations help visitors understand their options before booking.

A direct booking journey

Book Now and availability links connect visitors to the salon’s booking provider.

A personal introduction

Carli’s story, studio location, and Instagram link give visitors a fuller picture of the business.
