Cabrera Auto Services

A practical website that helps drivers explore services, plan a repair, and contact a neighborhood shop.

Visit the live website ↗ (opens in a new tab)

The business

Cabrera Auto Services serves Belmont Cragin and Chicago’s West Side. The website brings auto repair services, available vehicles, location details, and contact options into one place.

The design

The page structure follows the information a driver needs: what the shop does, where it is, how to call, and what to prepare before a visit. Clear service labels and neighborhood references make the business easy to understand.

Project details

Repair planning

An on-page estimator shows a sample parts and labor calculation, clearly identified as planning information rather than a confirmed quote.

Vehicles for sale

An inventory page gives available cars a dedicated place alongside the repair business.

Clear next steps

Phone links, directions, hours, and service-specific contact prompts help visitors reach the shop directly.
